# KCSE Robot app

Download the KCSE Robot app for your school:

- **Android phone:** [kcserobot.apk](https://github.com/FELIXRONOH/kcserobot-downloads/releases/latest/download/kcserobot.apk)
- **Windows computer (Windows 10 or later, 64-bit):** [kcserobot-setup.exe](https://github.com/FELIXRONOH/kcserobot-downloads/releases/latest/download/kcserobot-setup.exe)

After installing, open the app and sign in with your school account.
